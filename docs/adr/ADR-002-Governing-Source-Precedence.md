\# ADR-002 Governing Source Precedence



\## Status



Accepted



\## Decision



The reviewer shall apply requirements in this order:



1\. Regulatory and AHJ requirements

2\. Approved project deviations and Tech Gov decisions

3\. Project-specific requirements

4\. Engineering Bulletins

5\. Microsoft Master Specifications

6\. Programme BOD

7\. Approved MTS baseline

8\. Vendor submission

9\. Consultant comments



\## Consequences



Review findings shall reference the governing

source and applicable precedence.
