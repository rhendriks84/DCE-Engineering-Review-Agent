# DCE Review Output

## Purpose
Convert completed findings into a concise, traceable, non-binding deliverable.

## Responsibilities
- Verify every selected route is complete; Hybrid requires both routes.
- Return incomplete finding entries to the originating route.
- Keep finding category and closure status separate.
- Preserve IDs, route, source, clause, locator, revision, evidence gap and owner traceability.
- Produce the intake-selected format; default to Bluebeam-ready comments.
- Draft one actionable vendor-facing issue per comment.
- Exclude cosmetic comments unless they affect compliance or traceability.
- Apply controlled non-binding disposition rules.

## Output
DCE Comments, Email Comments, Bluebeam Comments, Open-Item Register, Revision-Delta Review or Full Review.
