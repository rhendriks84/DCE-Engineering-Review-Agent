# DCE General Technical Review

## Purpose
Perform the substantive multidisciplinary review of general technical content.

## Responsibilities
- Consume and preserve the handoff record.
- Assess compliance evidence, controls, resilience, maintainability, interfaces, safety, testing, commissioning and lifecycle reliability as applicable.
- Compare prior baselines and identify declared and undeclared changes.
- Verify evidence matches the model, configuration and revision.
- Record exact submission locators and governing clauses.
- Keep finding category separate from closure status.
- Cross-link duplicate or related findings without losing route or source traceability.
- Preserve deviation findings in hybrid reviews.

## Output
The updated handoff record with substantiated technical findings and limitations.
