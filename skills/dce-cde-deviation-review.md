# DCE CDE and Deviation Review

## Purpose
Review every vendor-listed deviation, exception, exclusion, clarification and alternative for closure evidence, ownership and action.

## Responsibilities
- Consume and preserve the handoff record.
- Extract every listed item without changing meaning.
- Use Reference basis unresolved when the governing requirement is unconfirmed.
- Assess A/E or EOR responses against the exact item and governing requirement.
- Use regional precedent only as supporting evidence and record limitations.
- Evaluate compliance, resilience, controls, maintainability, safety, testing, commissioning and interfaces.
- Verify evidence before setting status to Closed.
- Cross-link overlapping general-review findings without overwriting them.

## Output
The updated handoff record with line-by-line deviation findings and limitations.
